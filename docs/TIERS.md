### How to get it after you pay

An email with your download link (valid 7 days), a sha256 to check and your license key arrives within minutes of payment, at the address you ordered from. If it does not show up, check spam, then write to tanner@kodros.ai with your receipt. If the link expires, reply to the email for a new one. Then:

```bash
sha256sum meshvault-harness-pro-1.0.0.tar.gz     # macOS: shasum -a 256
meshvault pro install meshvault-harness-pro-1.0.0.tar.gz
```

Updates for 12 months arrive the same way. Refund within 14 days if the pack does not do what this page says.
